The 16/s row completed 97 correct DAGs over 13.484 s including drain, 7.194 DAG/s. The 4/s stability cell completed 480 correct DAGs over 120.164 s, 3.995 DAG/s, with maximum 835.0 ms. This demonstrates the stated 4/s bounded workload for 120 s; it does not establish maximum sustainable rate between 4 and 16/s or stability with an unbounded lineage store.

At 16/s, tracked coordinator CPU averaged 83.1% of one core, each Go worker 5.5–5.9%, generator 41.1%; peaks were 25.9 MB coordinator RSS, 23.6 / 22.9 MB worker RSS and 107.7 MB generator RSS. At 4/s stability, coordinator averaged 47.6%, Go workers 4.3 / 2.1%, generator 15.7%; peaks were 47.1 MB, 25.3 / 24.5 MB and 132.1 MB. These derive from 100 ms /proc snapshots and CLK_TCK=100. Worker Python subprocess CPU/RSS is excluded, so these are not aggregate runtime resource totals. Peer transfer counters increased 19 in the overloaded cell and 2 in the stability cell. A separate native hot-path profile is still needed before claiming the cause. Source shows full-state serialization/persistence as a candidate, not a proven cause.

Single controlled recovery observations retained exact result and retry 1 → 2. Assigned-worker loss recovered in 5.111 s after injection; coordinator restart recovered in 6.261 s. Each workload includes an intentional three-second installed slow task. Neither number is a recovery percentile or fault SLO. All owned processes exited and receipts survived cleanup.
